# Public links

share a recording or a running session with anyone, no sign-in: the dashboard's session or recording menu (**Share…**), the live view's session menu, `browser-daemon recording share ID`, `browser-daemon session share SESSION [--control]` (links expire after an hour unless told otherwise: `--expires 7d`, `--expires never`, or the dialog; the API's `expires_in_secs` absent means never; `unshare` ends it), `POST /api/shares {"kind": "recording"|"session", "id": ..., "expires_in_secs": ..., "control": false}`, `DELETE /api/shares/<id>`. A link is `<share URL>/share/<node>/<token>` with a 64-byte random token (86 characters); any node serves it and forwards to the node named in it, so one public name (`services.browser-daemon.shareUrl`, `--share-url`) reaches the whole mesh. A recording's page plays it (sound included, addresses cut down to their site); a session's page shows its front tab, about a picture a second, and with **control** (Chromium) also takes clicks, scrolling, typed text and a few named keys (Enter, Tab, arrows, ...), as input events: no DevTools connection, no address bar, no shortcuts. Sharing a recording keeps it. A link ends when it is unshared, expires, or its session or recording is gone. What is public is marked everywhere: a banner on the dashboard while anything is shared (**Manage** lists every link, with **Stop**), a **Public** chip on session cards and recordings, a **PUBLIC** pill in the live view, whose eye chip counts the other live views, the people on the public link and the automation clients. One dialog (dashboard and live view) makes a link and manages it: the link with **Copy** and its QR code (`GET /api/qr?text=...`, SVG), **Allow control**, the expiry (changes apply at once) and **Stop sharing** (two clicks). While a session is shared, the passkey prompt and the vault button say so (with control: someone on the link may have started the sign-in), without refusing anything. The reverse proxy must serve `/share/` without its login (only that path: the daemon's pages hand out a sign-in cookie); share pages carry `noindex`, `no-referrer` and a strict CSP, and cannot be embedded.
